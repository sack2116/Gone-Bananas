    How to play

    • ◀ ▶ Move with the arrow keys or the ◀ ▶ buttons
    • ▲ Jump with the up arrow, Space or JUMP
    • 🍌 Bananas tip toward the side you stand on. Stay in the middle or push the other way!
    • ⏳ Bananas turn brown and drop if you stay too long. Keep hopping!
    • ☁️ Falling? Press JUMP in the air for one emergency jump per life
    • ⚠️ Once you've reached a banana, landing back on the ground costs a life
    • ⏱ Beat the clock! If time runs out you keep your points, but the level isn't finished
    • 🐒 From level 3, Bruno the Bad Baboon throws coconuts. Watch for the ! and dodge!
    • 🪙 Coins are worth 1, 2 or 5. Every 20 coin points = ❤️ extra life
    • 🌴 Reach the top of the tree and eat the whole bunch of bananas. ¡Ñam!
    • ⭐ Get 3 stars: reach the top, don't fall, grab most coins
    • 🐢 Slow · 🐇 Medium · 🏎️ Fast: each world gets faster as you go
    • 🚀 Finish the jungle to unlock the Banana Galaxy: you float, the screen never stops, and ghost bananas blink in and out!
